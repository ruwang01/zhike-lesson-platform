# Login-page assets

The three Earth textures retain the resources used by the existing globe component, pinned to three-globe 2.45.6 and served locally:

- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three-globe@2.45.6/example/img/earth-blue-marble.jpg
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three-globe@2.45.6/example/img/earth-topology.png
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three-globe@2.45.6/example/clouds/clouds.png

The three login sound effects are from the user's login-page-handoff.zip. The review-only combined audio and reference image are not shipped.

Place coordinates use city centres from GeoNames. McMurdo coordinates use NSF: https://www.nsf.gov/od/opp/ail/mcmurdo-station
Local times use the IANA time zones in the handoff package and Intl.DateTimeFormat; no geolocation or weather service is used.
